Research Intern
Jan 2022 - Jun 2022

During my Master’s research internship in the Roucou Lab (Department of Biochemistry and Functional Genomics, University of Sherbrooke), I was responsible for designing and implementing a large-scale structural prediction pipeline to model over 900,000 non-canonical microproteins and integrate the resulting data into the OpenProt database.

Over a 6-month period, I:

  • Implemented and optimized MMseqs2 on high-performance computing infrastructure (Digital Research Alliance of Canada / formerly Compute Canada),

  • Generated multiple sequence alignments (MSAs) as inputs for structure prediction,

  • Deployed AlphaFold2 at scale using Apptainer/Singularity containers on the HPC environment,

  • Orchestrated and automated large-scale computational workflows,

  • Integrated validated predictions into OpenProt for community access.

Working in a primarily wet-lab biochemistry environment, I also played a key role in bridging the computational and experimental perspectives: I communicated bioinformatics concepts clearly, led project meetings, and presented visual summaries to help experimental researchers understand and leverage the large-scale structural insights generated. This work resulted in the publication of a peer-reviewed article in Nucleic Acids Research (NAR), where I am credited as first co-author.

Research intern - INSERM France
Apr 2021 - Jul 2021
Feb 2025 - Mar 2025

During my Master’s internship at INSERM (Paris, France), conducted shortly after the first COVID-19 lockdown, I contributed to the global effort against the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic through large-scale in-silico drug repurposing. My objective was to identify potential inhibitors of the Spike protein, specifically those capable of preventing its interaction with the human ACE2 receptor. I performed high-throughput virtual screening of FDA-approved compounds, evaluating and ranking candidate molecules for their ability to disrupt the Spike-ACE2 binding interface.

This work involved:

  • High-throughput screening and molecular docking workflows,
  • Filtering, scoring, and prioritizing candidate compounds,
  • Analyzing interaction interfaces at the structural level,
  • Contributing to the design of follow-up experimental validation strategies.
  • This experience strengthened my expertise in structure-based drug design and large-scale computational screening, while allowing me to contribute meaningfully to research efforts during a critical global health challenge.

Results : Delivered initial forecasts consistent with Météo-France predictions.
